Questions people ask first

How long until I get my photos?

A 10-photo sneak peek arrives in 48 hours. The full gallery arrives within 10 days, or 14 days for weddings.

How do I reserve a date?

Send the booking form below. A 30% deposit holds your date, and the balance is due on the day of the shoot.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. You can move your date once for free if you tell the studio at least 7 days before.

About

I started Stellar Lens in 2024 after years of shooting friends' weddings and family parties for free. People kept asking what I charged, so I made it a real studio.

My style is natural light and warm, true color. I arrive early, find the best light in the room, and stay out of the way so people forget the camera is there.

  • Started: 2024, Metro Manila
  • Kit: two mirrorless cameras, 35mm and 85mm lenses
  • Look: natural light, warm tones, light editing
  • Replies: within one day
